RESTAURANT PARIS SPHERE, THE CHIC THAT'S THE CAPITAL.

Located on rue la Boétie in the 8th arrondissement of Paris, the French restaurant Sphère is a new Parisian restaurant for the pleasure of taste and sight.
This restaurant in Paris is ideally located and represents Parisian gastronomy through its authenticity.
You may not know its name yet, but its refined, modern aesthetic already sets the tone.
In a zen-like, upscale ambience, you can enjoy top-flight dishes created by chef Tetsuya YOSHIDA.

THE RESTAURANT

The SPHERE gourmet restaurant plunges you into a unique atmosphere of luxury and sophistication in Paris intra muros. Evolving throughout the day, it's the ideal place to sit down, share and exchange while the neighborhood comes alive at lunchtime. At nightfall, it plunges visitors into a hushed atmosphere for intimate dining or a romantic moment as a couple.
