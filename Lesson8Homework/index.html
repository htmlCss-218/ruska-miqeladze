today i am goona review latest fashion trends.

1. Oversized Silhouettes: Baggy jeans, oversized blazers, and loose-fitting tops continue to dominate.
2. Y2K Revival: Nostalgic elements from the early 2000s, like low-rise jeans and crop tops, are back in style.
3. Bold Colors: Vibrant hues, particularly in unexpected combinations, are popular.
4. Cutouts and Asymmetry: Clothing with strategic cutouts or asymmetrical designs is trending.
5. Sustainable Fashion: Eco-friendly materials and upcycled designs are increasingly sought after.
6. Athleisure: Comfortable yet stylish sportswear remains a staple in everyday fashion.
7. Statement Accessories: Chunky jewelry and bold bags are key to elevating outfits.
